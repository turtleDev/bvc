How it works

STEP 1
Join the group
One tap — it's our whole clubhouse.
STEP 2
Get on the list
Every game post has a player list. Pay the host to claim a spot — once you're on the updated list, you're in.
STEP 3
Show up & play
We'll wave you onto a court in minutes.

Good to know

Your spot is confirmed when the host reposts the list with your name · paid but can't make it? Say so early so someone can take your place · rotate in fairly · beginners get extra touches, not eye-rolls.

About

We're a volunteer-run community of 400+ players keeping volleyball alive across Bangalore since 2025. No club fees, no committees, no legal entity — just people who love the game, organizing courts and welcoming whoever shows up.
