Terms & safety

Last updated 3 August 2026

The short version

If you read nothing else, read this. It applies to everyone who plays at a Bangalore Volleyball Club game.

English

Volleyball carries a real risk of injury. I am 18 or over, I am medically fit to play, and I choose to play voluntarily and at my own risk. BVC is a group of volunteer players — not a company, club or registered body — and provides no coaching, refereeing, medical care or insurance. I am responsible for my own health cover.

Who we are

Bangalore Volleyball Club ("BVC") is an informal, volunteer-run community of people who like volleyball. It is not a registered company, society, trust or partnership, and it is not a legal entity. Nobody is employed by it and nobody profits from it.

Games are organised by whichever member volunteers to host that day. A host is a fellow player doing a favour for the group — not a coach, an official, a referee, a trainer, or a medical professional, and not anyone's employee or agent.

Playing is at your own risk

Volleyball is a contact-adjacent, high-impact sport. Injuries happen in the ordinary course of play even when everyone is careful. Common ones include sprained ankles, jammed or broken fingers, knee and shoulder injuries, collisions with other players, and falls. Serious injury is less likely but possible.

By turning up and playing, you accept these risks voluntarily and agree that you are playing at your own risk. You are the best judge of your own fitness and ability — please use that judgement.

Conditions of playing

  • 18 and over only. We do not organise games for minors and cannot accept under-18 players.
  • Play only if you are fit to. Don't play injured, unwell, or while recovering from something that could be made worse. If you have a heart condition, a respiratory condition, or any medical issue that could be aggravated by hard exercise, get your doctor's clearance first.
  • No alcohol or intoxicants. Don't play after drinking or using anything that impairs your judgement or balance. Hosts can and will ask you to sit out.
  • Arrange your own health cover. BVC holds no insurance of any kind and does not cover medical costs.
  • Bring your own gear and look after your things. We're not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged belongings.
  • Warm up, and tell the host about anything relevant — an injury you're managing, a condition that might matter in an emergency, or an emergency contact we should know.

Venues

We play on courts hired from third-party venues. We don't own, build, maintain or inspect them beyond a basic look-over before play. The condition and safety of a court, its equipment, lighting, surface and surroundings are the venue operator's responsibility, and each venue's own rules apply while you're on their premises.

If you spot something unsafe, tell the host and the venue immediately rather than fixing it yourself.

Money

Joining the group is free. Courts are not, so players split what the venue charges.

What you send the host is your share of the court hire for that session — nothing more. It is not a membership fee, a ticket, or payment for a service. No one takes a cut, no one draws a salary, and the group does not aim to hold a surplus. Hosts keep venue receipts, and any player can ask to see what a session cost and what was collected.

If you've paid and can't make it, say so early so someone can take your place. Because the court is booked and paid for regardless, we can't generally refund a spot at short notice — but we'll always try to pass it on.

Code of conduct

The whole point of this group is that anyone can show up and get a game. That only works if everyone is decent to each other.

  • Treat every player with respect, whatever their skill, gender, age, caste, religion, language, nationality, disability, or sexual orientation.
  • Beginners get extra touches, not eye-rolls.
  • No harassment of any kind — sexual, verbal, physical, or online. No unwanted contact, comments about someone's body, or persistent messaging after someone has asked you to stop.
  • No aggressive, intimidating or dangerous play. No fighting.
  • Rotate in fairly and call your own faults honestly.
  • Respect venue staff, venue rules and neighbours.
  • Don't use the group to sell, spam, or recruit.

If something goes wrong: tell any host directly, or message a host privately if that's easier. We'll listen, keep it confidential as far as we reasonably can, and act. Depending on what happened, that can mean a conversation, a warning, or being removed from the group and barred from games. Serious matters — anything involving violence, or an offence — will be reported to the police, and we'll support anyone who wants to report something themselves.

Hosts can ask anyone to stop playing or leave, for safety or conduct reasons, without having to justify it on the spot.

If someone gets hurt

Stop play. Don't move anyone who may have a head, neck or back injury. Call an ambulance on 108 for anything serious, and stay with the injured person until help arrives. Tell the host so it can be written down while everyone still remembers it accurately.

Photos

We sometimes take photos and videos at games and use them on this site and in the group. If you'd rather not appear, tell the host — that's a completely normal request and we'll respect it. If a photo of you is already up and you want it taken down, ask and we'll remove it.

Privacy

This website has no forms, no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising. It sets no cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is your language choice, which never leaves your device.

We use Umami for basic, privacy-friendly visitor statistics — it's cookieless and doesn't track people across sites.

Everything else happens in WhatsApp, where your phone number and profile are visible to other group members. That's how WhatsApp groups work, and it's worth knowing before you join. We use your number to run games and nothing else. We don't sell, rent or share member information, and we'll remove you from the group and delete any list you're on if you ask.

Limits

To the fullest extent the law allows, the volunteers who organise and host BVC games are not liable for injury, loss or damage arising from your participation, from the condition of a venue, or from the acts of other players.

Nothing in these terms limits any liability that cannot lawfully be limited, and nothing here takes away a right you have under Indian law. Where any part of these terms is found to be unenforceable, the rest continues to apply.

These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka have jurisdiction.

Changes

We may update these terms. The current version always lives at this address, and we'll post a note in the group when something material changes. Continuing to play means the current version applies to you.
