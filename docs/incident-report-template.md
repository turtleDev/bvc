# Incident report

Copy this into a new dated file the **same day** as the incident — not the next
week. Fill in what you know; write "unknown" rather than guessing.

Use it for injuries needing more than a plaster, near-misses worth learning from,
conduct problems, and anything involving the venue or an ambulance.

Keep completed reports somewhere the hosts can find them and players can't
casually browse. Don't post them in the group.

---

**Notes on filling this in**

- Record facts, not conclusions. "The floor was wet near the baseline" is useful.
  "It was the venue's fault" is not, and could be used against you.
- Don't speculate about cause, and don't record admissions of fault — yours or
  anyone else's.
- If you're unsure whether something is worth reporting, report it. A file of
  boring near-miss reports is evidence of a group that takes safety seriously.
